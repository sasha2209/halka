Know who's on your ballot, before you mark it.

Credentials and declarations come from candidates' self-sworn ECI affidavits, via ADR / MyNeta, fetched by this project's own scraper and carrying a source link per candidate. Party symbols are shown only where the ECI reserves that symbol for that party; unrecognised parties and independents get a free symbol allotted per constituency, so those show as unconfirmed rather than guessed — check your ballot. Promises are auto-sorted into categories by a keyword classifier whose low-confidence guesses are flagged for review, never silently accepted. The compare tools rank nothing for you: the matrix sorts on whichever column you pick, and no single "best candidate" score is computed anywhere in this app.
Real data — sourced from ECI affidavits via ADR/MyNeta. A declared criminal case is a charge, not a conviction.
Sources: ECI affidavit archive via ADR/MyNeta, plus the news outlets cited per candidate.
