Know who's on your ballot, before you mark it.

Credentials and declarations come from candidates' self-sworn ECI affidavits, via ADR / MyNeta. The Background & news tab adds real reporting, cited per item, with disputes flagged rather than picked. Three constituencies are fully wired here, across two states: Digha, Bihar (all 11 candidates, concluded Nov 2025), and two bypolls voting the same day, 30 Jul 2026 — Bankipur, Bihar (Prashant Kishor's political debut) and Datia, Madhya Pradesh (vacant after the sitting MLA's fraud conviction). All three were hand-researched to prove the design generalizes beyond one state. A real scraper for MyNeta's structured data has been written and its parsing logic tested against real fetched content — see myneta_scraper.py — but it isn't deployed anywhere; scaling to India's ~4,700 constituencies needs that scraper running on a schedule somewhere with real network access, described in the accompanying strategy document. The "Compare on what matters to you" tool computes only from weights you set — never our judgment of who's better.
